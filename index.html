Made the home way

Gharcha Jevan means “a home-cooked meal” in Marathi, and that is the idea behind every plate: the day's fish, a freshly roasted masala and simple, filling rice-based meals.

  • Roasted masala

    A roasted spice blend gives the curries their colour and warmth.

  • Fish at the centre

    Fried, dry-cooked with masala, or simmered in a tangy curry.

  • Rice and bhakri

    Meals are eaten with steamed rice or soft rice-flour bhakri.
